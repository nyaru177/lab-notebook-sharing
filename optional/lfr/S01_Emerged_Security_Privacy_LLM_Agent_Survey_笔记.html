摘要 / Abstract

ENInspired by the rapid development of Large Language Models (LLMs), LLM agents have evolved to perform complex tasks. LLM agents are now extensively applied across various domains, handling vast amounts of data to interact with humans and execute tasks. The widespread applications of LLM agents demonstrate their significant commercial value; however, they also expose security and privacy vulnerabilities. At the current stage, comprehensive research on the security and privacy of LLM agents is highly needed. This survey aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the newly emerged privacy and security issues faced by LLM agents. We begin by introducing the fundamental knowledge of LLM agents, followed by a categorization and analysis of the threats.

中受大语言模型 (LLM) 快速发展的启发，LLM 智能体已经发展到可以执行复杂的任务。LLM 智能体现在广泛应用于各个领域，处理大量数据以与人类交互并执行任务。LLM智能体的广泛应用体现了其巨大的商业价值；然而，它们也暴露了安全和隐私漏洞。现阶段非常需要对LLM智能体的安全和隐私进行全面的研究。本综述旨在全面概述LLM智能体面临的新出现的隐私和安全问题。我们首先介绍LLM智能体的基础知识，然后对威胁进行分类和分析。

ENWe then discuss the impacts of these threats on humans, environment, and other agents. Subsequently, we review existing defensive strategies, and finally explore future trends. Additionally, the survey incorporates diverse case studies to facilitate a more accessible understanding. By highlighting these critical security and privacy issues, the survey seeks to stimulate future research towards enhancing the security and privacy of LLM agents, thereby increasing their reliability and trustworthiness in future applications.

中然后我们讨论这些威胁对人类、环境和其他智能体的影响。随后，我们回顾现有的防御策略，最后探讨未来的趋势。此外，该综述还纳入了不同的案例研究，以促进更容易理解。通过强调这些关键的安全和隐私问题，该综述旨在刺激未来的研究，以增强LLM智能体的安全和隐私，从而提高他们在未来应用中的可靠性和可信度。

1 引言 / Introduction

ENLarge Language Model (LLM) agents are sophisticated AI systems built upon large language models like GPT 4 [63], Claude 3 [6] and Llama 3 [5]. These agents leverage the vast amounts of text data on which they are trained to perform a variety of tasks, ranging from natural language understanding and generation to more complex activities such as decision-making, problem-solving, and interacting with users in a human-like manner [85]. LLM agents are accessible in numerous applications, including virtual assistants, customer service bots, and educational tools, due to their ability to understand and generate human language at an advanced level [88, 103]. The importance of LLM agents lies in their potential to transform various industries by automating tasks that require human-like understanding and interaction. They can enhance productivity, improve user experiences, and provide personalized assistance.

中大语言模型 (LLM) 智能体是基于 GPT 4 [63]、Claude 3 [6] 和 Llama 3 [5] 等大语言模型构建的复杂人工智能系统。这些智能体利用大量的文本数据来执行各种任务，从自然语言理解和生成到更复杂的活动，例如决策、解决问题以及以类似人类的方式与用户交互[85]。LLM 智能体可以在许多应用程序中使用，包括虚拟助理、客户服务机器人和教育工具，因为它们能够理解和生成高级人类语言 [88, 103]。LLM 智能体的重要性在于它们通过自动化需要类人理解和交互的任务来改变各个行业的潜力。它们可以提高生产力、改善用户体验并提供个性化帮助。

ENMoreover, their ability to learn from vast amounts of data enables them to continuously improve and adapt to new tasks, making them versatile tools in the rapidly evolving technological landscape [96]. To visualize how LLM agents can be integrated into practical scenarios, consider the example illustrated in Figure 1. This figure presents a pixelated virtual town to simulate an LLM agent application. The town includes gathering places found in real life, such as stores, offices, restaurants, museums, and parks. Each LLM agent acts as an independent resident, playing various roles and serving different functions, closely resembling the behaviors of real humans in a community. These agents can either be manually controlled to interact with specific characters and accomplish tasks, or they can operate autonomously, following their own plans and acquiring new knowledge through interactions within the virtual community.

中此外，它们从大量数据中学习的能力使它们能够不断改进和适应新任务，使它们成为快速发展的技术领域中的多功能工具[96]。为了直观地了解如何将 LLM 智能体集成到实际场景中，请考虑图 1 中所示的示例。该图展示了一个模拟 LLM 智能体应用程序的像素化虚拟城镇。该镇包括现实生活中的聚集场所，如商店、办公室、餐馆、博物馆和公园。每个LLM智能体都充当一个独立的居民，扮演着不同的角色并服务于不同的功能，与社区中真实人类的行为非常相似。这些智能体可以手动控制与特定角色交互并完成任务，也可以自主操作，遵循自己的计划并通过虚拟社区内的交互获取新知识。

The deployment of LLM agents has led to a wide user base and high commercial value due to their extensive application in various fields. Given that LLM agents are still in their early stages, their significant commercial and application values make them attractive targets for attackers. In addition, recent studies have quantitatively highlighted the risks faced by LLM agents. For example, SafeAgentBench [ 111] evaluated 16 representative LLM agents on 2,000 test cases in 349 environments and found that none of the tested LLM agents achieved an overall safety score above 60%, with some agents scoring below 20%.

中图 1.像素化虚拟城镇概述：每个标签都标识了一个特定的环境，例如商店、办公室、餐馆、博物馆和公园，其中每个LLM智能体都扮演着个性化的角色，模拟现实生活中的互动和任务。LLM智能体的部署因其在各个领域的广泛应用而带来了广泛的用户基础和较高的商业价值。鉴于 LLM 智能体仍处于早期阶段，其重要的商业和应用价值使它们成为攻击者有吸引力的目标。此外，最近的研究定量强调了LLM智能体面临的风险。例如，SafeAgentBench [111] 在 349 个环境中的 2000 个测试用例中评估了 16 个具有代表性的 LLM 智能体，发现没有一个被测试的 LLM 智能体的总体安全得分高于 60%，有些智能体得分低于 20%。

ENThis includes severe vulnerabilities such as misusing tools, and failing to recognize implicit safety risks. These findings highlight significant safety vulnerabilities in present LLM agents. Traditional machine learning (ML) security focuses on well-defined threat models such as adversarial example attacks that perturb inputs to cause mistakes, data poisoning attacks that compromise training datasets, and model extraction attacks that steal model parameters [100]. These threats typically target model prediction accuracy in constrained tasks, with impacts largely limited to misclassification or regression errors. While LLMs as advanced ML models inherit traditional vulnerabilities, they also introduce additional concerns through their generative capabilities and natural language processing.

中这包括严重的漏洞，例如滥用工具和未能识别隐含的安全风险。这些发现凸显了目前LLM智能体的重大安全漏洞。传统的机器学习 (ML) 安全性侧重于明确定义的威胁模型，例如扰乱输入导致错误的对抗性示例攻击、损害训练数据集的数据中毒攻击以及窃取模型参数的模型提取攻击 [100]。这些威胁通常针对受限任务中的模型预测准确性，其影响很大程度上仅限于错误分类或回归错误。虽然LLM作为高级机器学习模型继承了传统的漏洞，但它们也通过其生成能力和自然语言处理引入了额外的问题。

ENLLM agents, built on LLMs, further intensify these security challenges by adding complex reasoning, tool usage, and environmental interactions, creating cascading effects that extend beyond simple prediction errors and lead to real-world consequences. LLM agents inherit vulnerabilities from their underlying LLM foundation. For example, jailbreaking attacks can bypass the security and censorship features of LLMs, generating controversial responses [47]. This threat is inherited by LLM agents, enabling attackers to employ various methods to execute jailbreaking attacks on agents. However, LLM agents possess dynamic capabilities, such that their immediate responses can influence future decisions and actions, thereby posing more widespread risks. Beyond these inherited threats, the unique functionalities of LLM agents, such as their ability to think and utilize tools during task execution, expose them to specific attacks targeting agents.

中基于 LLM 的 LLM 智能体通过添加复杂的推理、工具使用和环境交互来进一步加剧这些安全挑战，产生级联效应，超越简单的预测错误并导致现实世界的后果。LLM 智能体从其底层 LLM 基础继承了漏洞。例如，越狱攻击可以绕过LLM的安全和审查功能，从而产生有争议的反应[47]。这种威胁由LLM智能体继承，使得攻击者能够利用各种方法对智能体执行越狱攻击。然而，LLM智能体拥有动态能力，因此他们的即时反应可以影响未来的决策和行动，从而带来更广泛的风险。除了这些继承的威胁之外，LLM 智能体的独特功能（例如它们在任务执行期间思考和利用工具的能力）使它们面临针对智能体的特定攻击。

ENFor example, Agent-SafetyBench [121] demonstrated that LLM agents often overlook safety constraints when they attempt tool-based operations, with an average safety score of only 38.5%. These issues include overlooking permissions, mismanaging tool interactions, and failing to consider implicit safety risks, leading to unsafe task outcomes. Similarly, failures could result in outcomes like leaking sensitive information, spreading misinformation, or even executing harmful actions unintentionally. Depending on the application domain of LLM agents, such attacks could pose serious threats to physical security, financial security, or overall system integrity. This paper categorizes the security threats faced by LLM agents into inherited LLM attacks and unique agent-specific threats. The threats inherited from LLMs can be further divided into technical vulnerabilities and intentional malicious attacks.

中例如，Agent-SafetyBench [121] 证明，LLM 智能体在尝试基于工具的操作时经常忽视安全约束，平均安全得分仅为 38.5%。这些问题包括忽视权限、工具交互管理不善以及未能考虑隐含的安全风险，从而导致不安全的任务结果。同样，失败可能会导致泄露敏感信息、传播错误信息，甚至无意中执行有害操作等结果。根据 LLM 智能体的应用领域，此类攻击可能会对物理安全、财务安全或整体系统完整性构成严重威胁。本文将 LLM 智能体面临的安全威胁分为继承的 LLM 攻击和独特的智能体特定威胁。LLM继承的威胁可以进一步分为技术漏洞和故意恶意攻击。

ENTechnical vulnerabilities include issues like hallucinations, catastrophic forgetting, and misunderstandings [ 96], which arise from the initial model creation and are influenced by the model’s structure. These vulnerabilities can cause users to observe incorrect results during prolonged use of LLM agents, affecting user trust and decision-making processes. For instance, AgentBench [52] demonstrated that LLMs achieve low success rates between 12% and 14% across real-world tasks, frequently exhibiting hallucination behaviors in knowledge-intensive scenarios, catastrophic forgetting in multi-turn interactions, and misunderstanding in complex reasoning scenarios. On the other hand, intentional malicious attacks deliberately exploit these vulnerabilities to achieve adversarial goals.

中技术漏洞包括幻觉、灾难性遗忘和误解等问题[96]，这些问题是在最初的模型创建过程中产生的，并受到模型结构的影响。这些漏洞可能导致用户在长时间使用LLM智能体时观察到不正确的结果，影响用户信任和决策过程。例如，AgentBench [52] 证明LLM在现实世界任务中的成功率较低，在 12% 到 14% 之间，在知识密集型场景中经常表现出幻觉行为，在多轮交互中出现灾难性遗忘，在复杂推理场景中出现误解。另一方面，故意恶意攻击故意利用这些漏洞来实现对抗目标。

ENRepresentative examples include jailbreaking attacks that bypass alignment safeguards to generate prohibited content, prompt injection attacks that insert malicious instructions into agent inputs to manipulate reasoning processes and outputs, data extraction attacks that induce agents to disclose sensitive training data or model parameters, and inference attacks, which aim to determine whether specific samples were part of the training set [ 108]. Building on these concepts, a variety of concrete attack methods have been developed in recent research and have demonstrated strong attack effectiveness, amplifying the severity of threats and highlighting the complexity and importance of protecting LLM agents. For the specific threats targeting LLM agents, we are inspired by the workflow of LLM agents, which involves agent perception, thought, and action [96].

中代表性的例子包括绕过对齐保护措施以生成禁止内容的越狱攻击、将恶意指令插入智能体输入以操纵推理过程和输出的提示注入攻击、诱导智能体泄露敏感训练数据或模型参数的数据提取攻击以及旨在确定特定样本是否是训练集的一部分的推理攻击[108]。基于这些概念，最近的研究开发了多种具体的攻击方法，并表现出强大的攻击有效性，放大了威胁的严重性，并凸显了保护 LLM 智能体的复杂性和重要性。对于针对 LLM 智能体的特定威胁，我们受到 LLM 智能体工作流程的启发，其中涉及智能体感知、思考和行动 [96]。

ENThe threats can be categorized into knowledge poisoning, output manipulation and functional manipulation. Knowledge poisoning involves contaminating the training data and knowledge base of the LLM agent, leading to the deliberate incorporation of malicious data by creator. This can easily deceive users with harmful information and even steer them towards malicious behavior. Output manipulation interferes with the content of the agent’s thought and perception stages, influencing the final output. This can cause users to receive biased or deceptive information, crafted to mislead them. Functional manipulation exploits the interfaces and tools used by LLM agents to perform unauthorized actions such as third-party data theft or executing malicious code.

中威胁可分为知识投毒、输出操纵和功能操纵。知识投毒涉及污染LLM智能体的训练数据和知识库，导致创建者故意掺入恶意数据。这很容易用有害信息欺骗用户，甚至引导他们进行恶意行为。输出操纵会干扰智能体的思维和感知阶段的内容，从而影响最终的输出。这可能会导致用户收到旨在误导他们的有偏见或欺骗性的信息。功能操纵利用 LLM 智能体使用的接口和工具来执行未经授权的操作，例如第三方数据盗窃或执行恶意代码。

Current studies mainly focus on attacks targeting LLMs, while lacking comprehensive reviews that discuss the security and privacy issues specific to the agents, which present more complex scenarios. The motivation for conducting this survey is to provide a comprehensive overview of the privacy and security issues associated with LLM agents, helping researchers to understand and mitigate the associated threats.

中LLM智能体的研究仍处于早期阶段。目前的研究主要集中在针对LLM的攻击，而缺乏讨论特定于智能体的安全和隐私问题的全面审查，这呈现出更复杂的场景。进行这项调查的动机是全面概述与 LLM 智能体相关的隐私和安全问题，帮助研究人员了解和减轻相关威胁。

ENGiven that LLM agents rely on LLMs as their core controllers for reasoning and planning, threats inherited from LLMs indirectly impact the security of LLM agents. These inherited threats are categorized into two types: those stemming from external malicious attacks and those arising from inherent vulnerabilities within the model itself. To better illustrate the impact pathways of these threats, we align them with the internal components of LLM agents introduced in Section 2.2 (e.g., LLM Engine, Instruction, Interface, Knowledge, and Memory). This mapping clarifies how technical vulnerabilities and malicious behaviors emerge and propagate from the foundational architecture of LLM agents. These two types of threats are distinct yet interconnected. Technical vulnerabilities arise from technical limitations during model development rather than malicious intent.

中鉴于LLM智能体依赖LLM作为推理和规划的核心控制器，从LLM继承的威胁间接影响LLM智能体的安全。这些继承的威胁分为两类：源自外部恶意攻击的威胁和源自模型本身固有漏洞的威胁。为了更好地说明这些威胁的影响路径，我们将它们与第 2.2 节中介绍的 LLM 智能体的内部组件（例如，LLM 引擎、指令、接口、知识和内存）保持一致。该映射阐明了技术漏洞和恶意行为如何从 LLM 智能体的基础架构中出现和传播。这两类威胁截然不同但又相互关联。技术漏洞源于模型开发过程中的技术限制，而不是恶意意图。

ENConversely, malicious attacks are intentional actions by external entities aimed at exploiting these vulnerabilities to compromise LLM agents. Despite their different origins and motives, technical vulnerabilities provide opportunities for attackers, enabling them to develop more sophisticated strategies and exposing LLM agents to various security and privacy risks.

中相反，恶意攻击是外部实体故意采取的行动，旨在利用这些漏洞来危害 LLM 智能体。尽管其起源和动机不同，但技术漏洞为攻击者提供了机会，使他们能够制定更复杂的策略，并使 LLM 智能体面临各种安全和隐私风险。

3.1.1 技术脆弱性 / Technical Vulnerabilities

ENDuring the training process of LLMs, limitations in the data and learning algorithms can introduce technical vulnerabilities [96], impeding the generation of accurate and reliable information. •Hallucination. The contemporary conception of hallucination in LLM agents, as delineated in the research by [ 36], is defined as instances where the output produced by these models is either inconsistent with or unreliable in relation to the input or source content provided. This issue primarily arises from the LLM Engine and Knowledge components, where errors occur during language generation or when retrieving and composing factual content. The phenomenon of hallucinations in LLM agents is a complex issue stemming from multiple stages of the model’s development process, including the nature of training data, the architectural design of the model, and the strategies employed during decoding. Several factors contribute to hallucination.

中在LLM的训练过程中，数据和学习算法的限制可能会引入技术漏洞[96]，阻碍生成准确可靠的信息。•幻觉。正如[36]的研究中所描述的，LLM智能体的幻觉的当代概念被定义为这些模型产生的输出与所提供的输入或源内容不一致或不可靠的实例。此问题主要源于 LLM 引擎和知识组件，在语言生成期间或检索和撰写事实内容时会出现错误。LLM 智能体中的幻觉现象是一个复杂的问题，源于模型开发过程的多个阶段，包括训练数据的性质、模型的架构设计以及解码过程中采用的策略。有几个因素会导致幻觉。

ENMisinformation and biases in both LLM Engine component training data and Knowledge component data can lead to the generation of inaccurate or biased outputs, which in turn result in different types of hallucinations [44]. Furthermore, flaws in the model’s architecture, such as limited directional representation and issues with attention mechanisms, along with exposure bias, further contribute to the occurrence of hallucinations [49]. Additionally, the randomness inherent in the decoding algorithms of these models can also lead to hallucinations, especially as this randomness increases [7].

中LLM Engine 组件训练数据和知识组件数据中的错误信息和偏差可能导致生成不准确或有偏差的输出，进而导致不同类型的幻觉 [44]。此外，模型架构中的缺陷，例如有限的方向表示和注意力机制问题，以及暴露偏差，进一步导致了幻觉的发生[49]。此外，这些模型的解码算法固有的随机性也可能导致幻觉，特别是当这种随机性增加时[7]。

EN•Catastrophic Forgetting. Catastrophic forgetting is a significant challenge encountered during the LLM agents’ fine-tuning and incontext learning processes. This phenomenon occurs when the model used by the LLM Engine component is fine-tuned on a small, specific dataset, causing it to overfit to this new data and, as a result, lose its previously acquired performance on other tasks [32]. This degradation weakens the Memory component’s ability to maintain coherent long-term context, as it relies on stable semantic representations. [56] discovers that catastrophic forgetting is significantly influenced by factors such as model size, architectural design, and the methods employed in continual fine-tuning and instruction tuning. As the scale of the model increases, catastrophic forgetting tends to become more severe.

中•灾难性遗忘。灾难性遗忘是 LLM 智能体微调和上下文学习过程中遇到的重大挑战。当 LLM Engine 组件使用的模型在小型特定数据集上进行微调时，会发生这种现象，导致其过度拟合新数据，从而失去之前在其他任务上获得的性能 [32]。这种退化削弱了记忆组件维持连贯的长期上下文的能力，因为它依赖于稳定的语义表示。 [56]发现灾难性遗忘受到模型大小、架构设计以及持续微调和指令调整所采用的方法等因素的显着影响。随着模型规模的增加，灾难性遗忘往往会变得更加严重。

ENMoreover, the architectural design of this underlying model, particularly those focusing on decoder-only structures, can influence the extent of catastrophic forgetting [117]. Additionally, during the process of continual instruction adjustment, the lack of effective regularization strategies or failure to balance new and old information in the LLM Engine component can accelerate forgetting, further exacerbating memory inconsistency [57]. Introducing more instructional tasks in continual training typically leads to more pronounced forgetting [67].

中此外，该底层模型的架构设计，特别是那些专注于仅解码器结构的架构设计，可能会影响灾难性遗忘的程度[117]。此外，在不断调整指令的过程中，LLM Engine组件中缺乏有效的正则化策略或未能平衡新旧信息会加速遗忘，进一步加剧记忆不一致[57]。在持续训练中引入更多的教学任务通常会导致更明显的遗忘[67]。

EN•Misunderstanding. Misunderstanding in LLM agents represents a notable challenge, particularly when they are tasked with responding to user inquiries or when they are integrated into a community for communication with other agents. This issue arises when LLM agents inadequately comprehend or inaccurately respond to the intentions or instructions conveyed by humans or other agents during interactions, potentially leading to inappropriate or dangerous behaviors that compromise safety and reliability. It primarily involves the LLM Engine and Instruction components, where ambiguous task prompts or limitations in semantic reasoning contribute to faulty interpretations. Investigations by [92] have revealed that the phenomenon of misunderstanding in LLM agents is shaped by a range of factors.

中•误解。LLM 智能体中的误解是一个显着的挑战，特别是当他们的任务是响应用户查询或当他们融入社区与其他智能体进行沟通时。当 LLM 智能体在交互过程中未能充分理解或不准确地响应人类或其他智能体传达的意图或指令时，就会出现此问题，可能导致损害安全性和可靠性的不适当或危险行为。它主要涉及 LLM 引擎和指令组件，其中模糊的任务提示或语义推理的限制会导致错误的解释。[92]的调查表明，LLM智能体的误解现象是由一系列因素造成的。

ENThese include the nature of the pre-training data used for the model in the LLM Engine component, the specific task settings assigned to the agents and conveyed through the Instruction component, and the contexts and scenarios in which interactions occur. The breadth and quality of the pre-training data fundamentally influence the LLMs’ capacity for language comprehension and their grasp of common sense knowledge. The designated task settings are pivotal in guiding the goal orientation and strategy selection of the LLMs. Additionally, the interaction environments and scenarios play a crucial role in determining the LLMs’ adaptability and effectiveness in collaborative contexts. Addressing these multifaceted aspects is essential for enhancing the understanding and response accuracy of LLM agents in diverse interactional settings.

中其中包括用于 LLM Engine 组件中的模型的预训练数据的性质、分配给智能体并通过指令组件传达的特定任务设置，以及发生交互的上下文和场景。预训练数据的广度和质量从根本上影响LLM的语言理解能力和常识知识的掌握。指定的任务设置对于指导LLM的目标定位和策略选择至关重要。此外，交互环境和场景在确定LLM在协作环境中的适应性和有效性方面发挥着至关重要的作用。解决这些多方面的问题对于增强 LLM 智能体在不同交互环境中的理解和响应准确性至关重要。

【补充笔记】幻觉、灾难性遗忘和误解通常不是攻击者直接制造的，但会让 Agent 在缺少恶意输入时也作出错误判断。它们属于可靠性问题，却能与工具权限结合并转化为安全事故。

EN•Tuned Instructional Attack. Tuned Instructional Attack in LLM agents is a category of attacks or manipulations that specifically target LLMs optimized through instruction-based fine-tuning. These attacks are designed to exploit the unique vulnerabilities that emerge when LLMs are finely tuned for specific tasks, subtly manipulating the model’s output to serve malicious purposes. Types of Tuned Instructional Attack: – Jailbreaking. Jailbreaking attacks in LLM agents refer to circumventing the model’s built-in restrictions and security measures, allowing agents to perform actions that are otherwise prohibited or to generate restricted content. These attacks manipulate the directives in the Instruction component and use the Interface component as the entry point to transmit crafted prompts, thereby bypassing the safety filters.

中•调整的教学攻击。LLM 智能体中的调整指令攻击是一类攻击或操作，专门针对通过基于指令的微调进行优化的 LLM。这些攻击旨在利用 LLM 针对特定任务进行微调时出现的独特漏洞，巧妙地操纵模型的输出以达到恶意目的。调整的教学攻击的类型： – 越狱。LLM 智能体中的越狱攻击是指规避模型的内置限制和安全措施，允许智能体执行原本禁止的操作或生成受限制的内容。这些攻击操纵指令组件中的指令，并使用接口组件作为入口点来传输精心设计的提示，从而绕过安全过滤器。

ENRecent advancements in techniques for jailbreaking attacks have demonstrated a range of innovative approaches. [114] introduces an automated mechanism for generating jailbreak prompts through Prompt Fuzzing, which utilizes seed prompts to generate a wider array of effective jailbreaking inputs. [ 16] presents MASTERKEY, a novel framework for analyzing and executing jailbreaking attacks on chatbots, using time-based analysis similar to SQL injections. It also features an automated system for generating effective jailbreak prompts by leveraging the learning capabilities of LLMs. [51] investigates a hierarchical genetic algorithm, AutoDan, specifically designed for structured discrete data like prompt text. This algorithm aims to refine the generation process of jailbreak prompts, ensuring their stealth and efficacy. – Prompt Injection.

中越狱攻击技术的最新进展已经展示了一系列创新方法。 [114]引入了一种通过提示模糊测试生成越狱提示的自动化机制，该机制利用种子提示来生成更广泛的有效越狱输入。 [16] 提出了 MASTERKEY，一种用于分析和执行聊天机器人越狱攻击的新颖框架，使用类似于 SQL 注入的基于时间的分析。它还具有一个自动化系统，可利用LLM的学习能力生成有效的越狱提示。 [51] 研究了一种分层遗传算法 AutoDan，专门为结构化离散数据（如提示文本）而设计。该算法旨在细化越狱提示的生成过程，确保越狱提示的隐蔽性和有效性。 – 及时注射。

ENPrompt injection attacks are intended to mislead the LLM agents by introducing malicious or unintended content into the prompts, causing agents to produce outputs that diverge from their training data and original purpose. This method involves crafting input prompts to bypass the model’s content filters or to elicit undesirable outputs. Similar to jailbreaking attacks, these attacks exploit the Instruction component by crafting the prompts that agents must follow, and rely on the Interface component to deliver those adversarial inputs, thereby bypassing content filters and eliciting undesirable outputs. [27] has highlighted concerns about potential new vulnerabilities, especially with LLMs accessing external resources, and demonstrated various prompt injection techniques.

中提示注入攻击旨在通过在提示中引入恶意或非预期内容来误导 LLM 智能体，导致智能体产生与其训练数据和原始目的不同的输出。此方法涉及精心设计输入提示以绕过模型的内容过滤器或引出不需要的输出。与越狱攻击类似，这些攻击通过制作智能体必须遵循的提示来利用指令组件，并依赖接口组件来传递这些对抗性输入，从而绕过内容过滤器并引发不需要的输出。 [27] 强调了对潜在新漏洞的担忧，特别是LLM访问外部资源时，并演示了各种提示注入技术。

ENSubstantial research [93] has focused on automating the identification of semantic payloads in prompt injections. [53] introduces HOUYI, an innovative black-box prompt injection attack methodology targeting service providers integrated with LLMs. HOUYI utilizes LLMs to infer the semantics of the target application based on user interactions and employs diverse strategies to construct the injected prompts. •Data Extraction Attack. Data extraction attacks are defined as efforts by adversaries to extract sensitive information or key insights from LLM agents or their underlying data, such as model gradients, training data, and even prompts, or confidential content. These attacks probe the model used by the LLM Engine component, whose parameters may contain private text, as well as the agent’s Knowledge and Memory components, and rely on the Interface component to send carefully crafted queries that extract hidden content.

中大量研究 [93] 集中在自动识别提示注入中的语义负载。 [53]介绍了HOUYI，一种创新的黑盒提示注入攻击方法，针对与LLM集成的服务提供商。 HOUYI 利用 LLM 根据用户交互推断目标应用程序的语义，并采用不同的策略来构建注入的提示。•数据提取攻击。数据提取攻击被定义为攻击者从 LLM 智能体或其底层数据中提取敏感信息或关键见解的行为，例如模型梯度、训练数据，甚至提示或机密内容。这些攻击探测 LLM 引擎组件使用的模型，其参数可能包含私有文本，以及智能体的知识和内存组件，并依赖接口组件发送精心设计的查询来提取隐藏内容。

ENRecent research has introduced innovative methods for extracting different types of data from LLM agents. [79] presents a method called Data-Free Model Extraction (DFME), which allows for replicate the target models using only the target’s black-box predictions, without the need for access to the original training data. [ 12] conducts a data extraction attack on GPT-2’s training data, extracting personally identifiable information, code, and UUIDs.

中最近的研究引入了从LLM智能体中提取不同类型数据的创新方法。 [79]提出了一种称为无数据模型提取（DFME）的方法，该方法允许仅使用目标的黑盒预测来复制目标模型，而不需要访问原始训练数据。 [12]对GPT-2的训练数据进行数据提取攻击，提取个人身份信息、代码和UUID。

ENThe attack strategy consisted of producing a large volume of prefixed text, sorting it by certain metrics, removing duplicates, and manually reviewing the top results to check for memorization, confirmed by online searches and querying OpenAI. [ 62] introduces PrivAgent, a black-box red-teaming framework leveraging reinforcement learning to automate adversarial prompt generation for LLM privacy leakage.

中攻击策略包括生成大量前缀文本，按某些指标对其进行排序，删除重复项，并手动检查最前面的结果以检查记忆情况，并通过在线搜索和查询 OpenAI 进行确认。 [38]已经证明了从LLM中提取培训数据的可行性，其中可能包含敏感的个人或私人信息。 [62] 介绍了 PrivAgent，这是一个黑盒红队框架，利用强化学习自动生成针对 LLM 隐私泄露的对抗性提示。

EN•Inference Attack. Although inference attacks share certain resemblances with data extraction attacks, they differ significantly in their objectives and emphasis. Data extraction attacks specifically aim to obtain the training data directly. In contrast, inference attacks estimate the probability that a particular data sample was part of the training dataset of LLM agents. These attacks primarily target the LLM Engine component, leveraging the memorization behavior of its underlying model to distinguish training samples from unseen data. They also rely on the Interface component to deliver finely tuned queries, enabling adversaries to observe model confidence or response likelihoods that signal membership information.

中•推理攻击。尽管推理攻击与数据提取攻击有某些相似之处，但它们的目标和重点有很大不同。数据提取攻击的具体目标是直接获取训练数据。相反，推理攻击估计特定数据样本是 LLM 智能体训练数据集一部分的概率。这些攻击主要针对 LLM 引擎组件，利用其底层模型的记忆行为来区分训练样本和看不见的数据。他们还依靠接口组件来提供微调的查询，使对手能够观察模型置信度或表示成员资格信息的响应可能性。

ENSince the rapid development of LLMs, the concern over inference attacks targeting these models has increased. Research [24] points out that existing membership inference attacks fail to reveal the privacy risks of LLMs. To counter this issue, a membership inference attack is introduced based on Self-calibrated Probabilistic Variation (SPV-MIA). This method utilizes the concept of memorization to create a more reliable signal for membership inference and introduces a novel self-prompt technique for effectively extracting reference datasets from LLMs. Their extensive testing shows that SPV-MIA outperforms existing approaches. Following this, study [42] proposes a user inference attack method that uses a likelihood ratio test statistic against a reference model. They evaluate this method on the GPT-Neo LLMs across various data domains, providing insights into what makes users more vulnerable to these attacks. Their findings also indicate that minimal data alterations can significantly increase vulnerability.

中随着LLM的快速发展，人们对针对这些模型的推理攻击的担忧有所增加。研究[24]指出，现有的成员资格推断攻击未能揭示LLM的隐私风险。为了解决这个问题，引入了基于自校准概率变异（SPV-MIA）的成员推理攻击。该方法利用记忆的概念为隶属推断创建更可靠的信号，并引入一种新颖的自我提示技术来有效地从LLM中提取参考数据集。他们的广泛测试表明 SPV-MIA 优于现有方法。随后，研究[42]提出了一种用户推理攻击方法，该方法使用针对参考模型的似然比检验统计量。他们在不同数据域的 GPT-Neo LLM 上评估了这种方法，深入了解是什么让用户更容易受到这些攻击。他们的研究结果还表明，最小的数据更改会显着增加漏洞。

【补充笔记】指令攻击、数据抽取和推断攻击主要利用模型的指令遵循与信息记忆能力。进入 Agent 场景后，攻击者不仅想得到异常文本，还可能借模型继承的权限访问文件、接口或私人数据。

4 威胁的影响 / The Impact of Threats

ENRecent studies emphasize the substantial impact of LLM agents on society and technological advancement, offering users expedited access to information, facilitating learning and knowledge exploration. However, as detailed in Section 3, numerous threats specifically targeting LLM agents have been identified, highlighting their vulnerability to malicious activities. The successful execution of such threats against LLM agents can lead to a spectrum of side effects. These not only compromise the privacy and security of individuals but also disrupt digital ecosystems and can extend harm to the physical environment and other agents in the virtual community.

中最近的研究强调了LLM智能体对社会和技术进步的重大影响，为用户提供了快速获取信息、促进学习和知识探索的机会。然而，正如第 3 节中详述的，已经发现了许多专门针对 LLM 智能体的威胁，凸显了它们容易受到恶意活动的影响。针对LLM智能体的此类威胁的成功执行可能会导致一系列副作用。这些不仅损害个人的隐私和安全，还会破坏数字生态系统，并可能扩大对物理环境和虚拟社区中其他主体的危害。

中考虑到人类用户是智能体社会的成员，他们与基于LLM的智能智能体的交互涉及广泛的信息交换。这一过程中固有的风险不容忽视。恶意智能体利用其表面上值得信赖的外表，可能会欺骗用户、泄露个人信息或给出误导性的响应。此外，这些恶意智能体可能会被用作进行网络攻击的工具，

中接受过网络数据培训的LLM智能体会产生隐私问题，其中通常包括个人信息[43]。通过推理攻击[42]和数据提取[12]等技术，对手可以利用这些模型侵犯个人隐私。此外，恶意 LLM 智能体还可以诱骗用户与攻击者共享信息。这种暴露有利于社会工程策略，使攻击者能够利用被盗的地址、电子邮件和电话号码等信息实施网络钓鱼诈骗并劫持个人账户，从而威胁财务安全。

ENLLM agents, as intelligent conversational robots capable of answering a wide range of questions, pose a risk if their outputs include manipulated biases or illicit content, such as the dissemination of false information and rumors, potentially leading to adverse impacts on public discourse [17, 29]. 中LLM 智能体作为能够回答广泛问题的智能会话机器人，如果其输出包含被操纵的偏见或非法内容（例如传播虚假信息和谣言），则会带来风险，可能会对公共话语产生不利影响 [17, 29]。此类活动可能扭曲公众认知，甚至操纵舆论，加剧社会矛盾、煽动不满情绪，威胁社会稳定。因此，恶意智能体挑战了社会管理和舆论塑造的框架，其影响超出了技术领域，延伸到了社会和心理层面。

4.1.4 促进网络攻击 / Facilitating Cyber-Attack Techniques

Malicious agents, equipped with advanced cyber attack knowledge, can enable novices to generate harmful scripts or software [ 21]. This democratization of cyber attack tools amplifies the threat landscape, as illustrated by agents that teach the creation and modification of malicious code.

中一个被忽视的危险是实施网络攻击的准入门槛降低。恶意智能体配备了先进的网络攻击知识，可以让新手生成有害的脚本或软件[21]。网络攻击工具的民主化放大了威胁形势，正如教授恶意代码创建和修改的智能体所说明的那样。

4.2 对环境的影响 / Impact on Environment

ENIn today’s increasingly digital and interconnected world, the term ‘environment’ encompasses both physical surroundings and digital systems that LLM agents interact with. These agents operate in virtual spaces and control real-world services via embodied AI and industrial control systems. Although this integration improves efficiency, it also introduces new risks, as malicious agents can threaten safety, the economy, ecosystems, and societal stability.

中在当今日益数字化和互联的世界中，“环境”一词涵盖了LLM智能体与之交互的物理环境和数字系统。这些智能体在虚拟空间中运行，并通过具体的人工智能和工业控制系统控制现实世界的服务。尽管这种集成提高了效率，但也带来了新的风险，因为恶意智能体可能会威胁安全、经济、生态系统和社会稳定。

【补充笔记】Agent 对环境的影响是它与普通聊天模型的关键区别。文件修改、数据库写入、邮件发送、代码执行和物理设备控制都可能产生不可逆后果，因此高风险动作需要权限约束与确认机制。

4.2.1 数据篡改与误操作 / Data Tampering and Misoperation

ENWhen malicious agents are placed within systems that control critical infrastructure like industry, transportation, energy, and environmental monitoring [83], they can cause malfunctions in industrial control systems by tampering with critical operational data, such as temperature and pressure indicators. This can lead to equipment damage, production halts, and even severe infrastructure destruction, ecological damage, and loss of human life and property.

中当恶意智能体被放置在控制工业、交通、能源和环境监测等关键基础设施的系统中时[83]，它们可能会通过篡改关键操作数据（例如温度和压力指示器）来导致工业控制系统出现故障。这可能导致设备损坏、生产停顿，甚至严重的基础设施破坏、生态破坏、人员生命财产损失。

4.2.2 物理安全威胁 / Physical Safety Threats

ENRecent studies have begun to explore embodied AI with LLM [ 90], capable of understanding and generating natural language, with physical forms or direct connections to physical systems, enabling them to perform tasks in the physical world. Malicious agents have the potential to control robots or other Embodied AI devices that interact with humans, performing hazardous actions that directly threaten human safety.

中最近的研究已经开始探索具有LLM的嵌入式人工智能[90]，它能够理解和生成自然语言，具有物理形式或与物理系统的直接连接，使它们能够在物理世界中执行任务。恶意智能体有可能控制机器人或其他与人类交互的嵌入式人工智能设备，执行直接威胁人类安全的危险行为。

4.2.3 网络安全风险扩散 / Cybersecurity Risk Proliferation

ENRegarding the impact on humans, malicious LLM agents lower the technical barrier for writing and implementing malicious code, directly enabling ordinary users, even novices lacking advanced cyberattack skills, to easily create and deploy harmful scripts and software [21]. This change directly expands the target group of cyber threats, increasing the risk of regular users becoming potential victims. A deeper analysis reveals that this direct impact on individual users indirectly affects the entire cyber environment and societal infrastructure. As malicious software and scripts become more widespread and accessible, the entire cybersecurity system is jeopardized, not only endangering cybersecurity itself but also potentially affecting various socioeconomic activities that rely on these networks’ normal operation.

中就对人类的影响而言，恶意LLM智能体降低了编写和实施恶意代码的技术门槛，直接使普通用户，甚至缺乏高级网络攻击技能的新手，可以轻松创建和部署有害脚本和软件[21]。这一变化直接扩大了网络威胁的目标群体，增加了普通用户成为潜在受害者的风险。更深入的分析表明，这种对个人用户的直接影响间接影响了整个网络环境和社会基础设施。随着恶意软件和脚本变得更加广泛和容易获取，整个网络安全系统受到威胁，不仅危及网络安全本身，还可能影响依赖这些网络正常运行的各种社会经济活动。

4.3 对其他智能体的影响 / Impact on Other Agents

ENTo simulate the feedback of communication and interaction among individuals within human communities in the real world, certain studies [48, 66] have established communities powered by LLM engines. These LLM agents within the communities are endowed with characteristics such as personality, knowledge, and memory, as discussed in Section 2.2, enabling autonomous interaction with the environment and other agents. When faced with threats, agents manipulated with malicious intent can inflict significant harm on other members of the community.

中为了模拟现实世界中人类社区内个体之间的沟通和互动的反馈，某些研究[48, 66]建立了由LLM引擎驱动的社区。社区内的这些 LLM 智能体被赋予了个性、知识和记忆等特征，如第 2.2 节中讨论的那样，能够与环境和其他智能体进行自主交互。当面临威胁时，恶意操纵的智能体可能会对社区的其他成员造成重大伤害。

4.3.1 信息失真与误导 / Information Distortion and Misleading

ENExtensive research has highlighted the role of LLM agents in negotiation and deceptive gaming scenarios [37, 87], which is a cause for concern. LLM agents may intentionally alter the information they disseminate to achieve hidden objectives. However, interactions between these agents and others can trigger and disseminate incorrect information, leading to “explosive spread” of misinformation, posing a considerable threat to community stability. If information dissemination can be maliciously manipulated, it could detrimentally affect trust, communication efficiency, and collaborative work among agents.

中广泛的研究强调了 LLM 智能体在谈判和欺骗性游戏场景中的作用 [37, 87]，这是一个令人担忧的问题。LLM 智能体可能会故意改变他们传播的信息以实现隐藏的目标。这种行为会对社区内的其他智能体产生重大影响，因为在正常情况下，仁慈的智能体会将通过感知和交流获得的信息存储在他们的记忆中。然而，这些智能体与其他人之间的互动可能会触发和传播错误信息，导致错误信息的“爆炸式传播”，对社区稳定构成相当大的威胁。如果信息传播可能被恶意操纵，则可能会对智能体之间的信任、通信效率和协作工作产生不利影响。

4.3.2 决策操纵 / Manipulation of Decision-Making

ENGiven the exceptional reasoning and decision-making abilities demonstrated by LLM agents in complex interactive environments, the potential for malicious agents to disrupt these processes becomes a significant concern. This influence can extend to various aspects of the community, including resource distribution, task allocation, and external interaction strategies.

中鉴于 LLM 智能体在复杂的交互环境中表现出卓越的推理和决策能力，恶意智能体破坏这些过程的可能性成为一个重大问题。通过传播精心设计的信息，这些智能体可以影响其他智能体的决策过程，甚至控制它们做出服务于恶意智能体目的的决策[31]。这种影响力可以延伸到社区的各个方面，包括资源分配、任务分配、外部互动策略等。

4.3.3 安全威胁 / Security Threats

ENIn some instances, malicious agents may disseminate harmful information or execute dangerous operations, directly threatening the safety of community members or data security [13]. This can lead to disorder within the entire community, making it difficult to manage and requiring significant effort to restore.

中在某些情况下，恶意智能体可能会传播有害信息或执行危险操作，直接威胁社区成员的安全或数据安全[13]。例如，恶意主体通过诱导其他主体做出不安全行为、故意传播旨在破坏社区结构的恶意代码、传播带有偏见的言论等，可以使社区内的正常主体逐渐同化，成为输出带有偏见和恶意信息的实体。这可能会导致整个社区混乱，难以管理，需要付出巨大的努力才能恢复。

5.3.1 知识投毒防御 / Defense on Knowledge Poisoning

EN[ 10] proposes a new method for detecting and filtering poisonous data in the training sets of supervised learning models. It specifically utilizes data provenance to identify groups of data with a high correlation in their likelihood of being poisoned. This innovative approach aids in the effective identification and removal of malicious data. [101] presents ParaFuzz, a novel framework for detecting poisoned samples at test time in LLMs, leveraging the interpretability of model predictions. The effectiveness of PARAFUZZ heavily depends on the specific prompts used with ChatGPT, which is employed to ensure highquality paraphrasing. To optimize the detection process, the study adopts fuzzing to develop precise paraphrase prompts. These prompts are designed to effectively neutralize backdoor triggers while preserving the semantic integrity of the text.

中[10]提出了一种检测和过滤监督学习模型训练集中有毒数据的新方法。它特别利用数据来源来识别与中毒可能性高度相关的数据组。这种创新方法有助于有效识别和删除恶意数据。 [101] 提出了 ParaFuzz，这是一种利用模型预测的可解释性在 LLM 测试时检测中毒样本的新颖框架。PARAFUZZ 的有效性在很大程度上取决于与 ChatGPT 一起使用的特定提示，ChatGPT 用于确保高质量的释义。为了优化检测过程，该研究采用模糊测试来开发精确的释义提示。这些提示旨在有效地消除后门触发器，同时保留文本的语义完整性。

ENThere is still a significant gap in research focused on developing efficient defense strategies to protect LLMs from knowledge poisoning attacks [15]. Furthermore, empirical evidence indicates that LLMs are increasingly susceptible to these attacks. Current defense mechanisms, such as filtering data or reducing model capacity, provide only limited protection and often result in decreased test accuracy [82]. Besides technical solutions, specialized security strategies for AI systems are crucial, including verifying model sources, limiting sensitive training data, and detecting and mitigating attacks. Regular security reviews and risk assessments should also be conducted to identify and address new threats, ensuring AI systems are secure and up-to-date [19].

中专注于开发有效防御策略以保护LLM免受知识投毒攻击的研究仍然存在重大差距[15]。此外，经验证据表明LLM越来越容易受到这些攻击。当前的防御机制，例如过滤数据或减少模型容量，只能提供有限的保护，并且通常会导致测试准确性下降[82]。除了技术解决方案之外，人工智能系统的专门安全策略也至关重要，包括验证模型来源、限制敏感训练数据以及检测和减轻攻击。还应进行定期安全审查和风险评估，以识别和解决新威胁，确保人工智能系统安全且最新[19]。

5.3.2 输出操纵防御 / Defense on Output Manipulation

ENTo prevent individual LLM agents from being deceived by other agents, it is advisable to enhance their detection capabilities to determine whether they have encountered deception. [23] investigates using BERT with some added attention layers to detect deception in text, particularly in the context of Italian dialogues. This study establishes new methods for identifying deception and discusses how various contexts and semantic information contribute to detecting deceptive content. Moreover, inspired by human recursive thinking in the Avalon game, [87] introduces Recursive Contemplation (ReCon), a framework designed to enhance LLMs’ ability to detect and counter deceptive information. ReCon employs formulation, which generates initial thoughts and speech, and refinement, which improves these outputs.

中为了防止个别LLM智能体被其他智能体欺骗，建议增强他们的检测能力，以确定他们是否遇到了欺骗。 [23] 研究使用带有一些附加注意层的 BERT 来检测文本中的欺骗，特别是在意大利语对话的背景下。这项研究建立了识别欺骗的新方法，并讨论了各种上下文和语义信息如何有助于检测欺骗性内容。此外，受到阿瓦隆游戏中人类递归思维的启发，[87]引入了递归思考（ReCon），这是一个旨在增强LLM检测和反击欺骗性信息的能力的框架。ReCon 采用公式化（生成初始想法和语音）和细化（改进这些输出）。

ENIt also includes two perspective transitions, aiding LLMs in understanding others’ mental states and how others perceive their own mental states. In addition, [ 98] has developed a benchmarking framework called MAgIC, designed to evaluate LLMs in multi-agent environments. It utilizes games and game theory scenarios to test models on reasoning, cooperation, and adaptability. The research employs Probabilistic Graphical Modeling (PGM) to enhance models’ capabilities in handling complex social interactions. Finally, to address privacy concerns in LLM conversational agents, [9] proposes AirGapAgent, which mitigates output manipulation by restricting access to only task-relevant data. It employs data minimization to prevent sensitive information exposure and introduces context isolation to prevent unauthorized access and manipulation. Additionally, a request escalation mechanism ensures user oversight in uncertain cases, balancing privacy and functionality.

中它还包括两个视角转换，帮助LLM了解他人的心理状态以及他人如何看待自己的心理状态。此外，[98]还开发了一个名为 MAgIC 的基准测试框架，旨在评估多智能体环境中的 LLM。它利用游戏和博弈论场景来测试模型的推理、合作和适应性。该研究采用概率图形建模（PGM）来增强模型处理复杂社交互动的能力。最后，为了解决 LLM 会话智能体中的隐私问题，[9] 提出了 AirGapAgent，它通过限制仅访问与任务相关的数据来减轻输出操纵。它采用数据最小化来防止敏感信息暴露，并引入上下文隔离来防止未经授权的访问和操纵。此外，请求升级机制可确保用户在不确定情况下进行监督，平衡隐私和功能。

5.3.3 功能操纵防御 / Defense on Functional Manipulation

ENGiven the emergence of functional manipulation as a new risk associated with the deployment of LLM agents, research on this specific threat remains limited. Thus, proactive security measures are essential. When using third-party LLM agents, it is crucial to protect personal privacy and be wary of excessive personal data requests by third parties. Users should limit data sharing, especially avoiding sensitive or personally identifiable information during interactions with LLM agents. Additionally, understanding and utilizing the data protection settings offered by LLM agents is vital. Adjusting privacy settings helps control what data can be collected and processed.

中鉴于功能操纵作为与部署 LLM 智能体相关的新风险，对这种特定威胁的研究仍然有限。因此，主动的安全措施至关重要。使用第三方LLM智能体时，保护个人隐私并警惕第三方过多的个人数据请求至关重要。用户应限制数据共享，尤其是在与 LLM 智能体交互期间避免敏感或个人身份信息。此外，了解和利用 LLM 智能体提供的数据保护设置至关重要。调整隐私设置有助于控制可以收集和处理的数据。

ENChoosing providers with a strong reputation and transparency is also recommended, as these providers should have clear data usage and privacy protection policies along with a robust security track record [122]. To further address the challenges posed by functional manipulation, the introduction of the ToolEmu [72] framework represents a significant advancement. This framework employs a language model to emulate tool execution, which allows for extensive and scalable testing of LLM agents across diverse scenarios and toolsets. Together with an LLM-based automatic safety evaluator, ToolEmu facilitates the identification and quantification of risks by examining potential failures and subsequent consequences. This method provides a dynamic alternative to traditional static sandbox evaluations, enhancing the ability to detect and mitigate high-stakes, long-tail risks effectively.

中还建议选择具有良好声誉和透明度的提供商，因为这些提供商应具有明确的数据使用和隐私保护政策以及强大的安全跟踪记录[122]。为了进一步解决功能操作带来的挑战，ToolEmu [72] 框架的引入代表了重大进步。该框架采用语言模型来模拟工具执行，从而允许跨不同场景和工具集对 LLM 智能体进行广泛且可扩展的测试。ToolEmu 与基于LLM的自动安全评估器一起，通过检查潜在故障和后续后果来促进风险的识别和量化。该方法为传统静态沙箱评估提供了动态替代方案，增强了有效检测和缓解高风险、长尾风险的能力。

ENIn addition, the Situational Awareness Uncertainty Propagation (SAUP) [124] framework offers a novel defense mechanism by propagating uncertainty throughout the multi-step reasoning process of LLM agents. Unlike traditional methods that focus solely on single-step uncertainty, SAUP incorporates situational awareness by assigning weights to uncertainties at each step, enabling real-time detection of functional manipulation attempts. By identifying logical deviations and monitoring accumulated uncertainty, SAUP enhances the reliability of LLM agents and mitigates the risk of executing malicious operational patterns. Finally, [8] proposes an initial set of safety standards as an essential first step in industry self-regulation.

中此外，态势感知不确定性传播（SAUP）[124]框架通过在 LLM 智能体的多步骤推理过程中传播不确定性，提供了一种新颖的防御机制。与仅关注单步不确定性的传统方法不同，SAUP 通过为每一步的不确定性分配权重来整合态势感知，从而能够实时检测功能操作尝试。通过识别逻辑偏差和监控累积的不确定性，SAUP 增强了 LLM 智能体的可靠性并降低了执行恶意操作模式的风险。最后，[8] 提出了一套初始安全标准，作为行业自律的重要第一步。

ENThese standards include pre-deployment risk assessments, external reviews of model behavior, the use of risk assessments to inform deployment decisions, and monitoring and responding to new information about model functionality post-deployment. This approach contributes valuable insights to the broader discussion on balancing public safety risks with the benefits of innovation in AI development. 6.1.2 MLLM 智能体安全与隐私 / Security and Privacy of MLLM Agent

ENThe development of embodied agents capable of interacting with the real world has become a highly active area of research. However, MLLM agents also present several security vulnerabilities, one of which is the phenomenon of multimodal hallucinations. Fig. 18. Illustration of multimodal hallucinations . Given an image, an MLLM agent outputs a corresponding response with two primary forms Unlike language hallucinations, multimodal hallucinations refer to the phenomenon where the output descriptions generated by MLLMs are inconsistent with the actual content of images [110], as shown in Figure 18. These phenomena manifest in two primary forms [45]: one involves generated content that includes objects which are inconsistent with or absent from the target image [50, 116]; the other, a more complex form, encompasses holistic misrepresentations of entire scenes or environments [76].

中开发能够与现实世界交互的实体智能体已成为一个高度活跃的研究领域。然而，MLLM 智能体也存在一些安全漏洞，其中之一就是多模态幻觉现象。图 18.多模式幻觉的例证。给定图像，MLLM智能体输出具有两种主要形式的相应响应。与语言幻觉不同，多模态幻觉是指MLLM生成的输出描述与图像的实际内容不一致的现象[110]，如图18所示。这些现象以两种主要形式表现出来[45]：一种涉及生成的内容，其中包含与目标图像不一致或不存在的对象[50, 116]；另一种涉及生成的内容，其中包含与目标图像不一致或不存在的对象[50, 116]。另一种是更复杂的形式，包含对整个场景或环境的整体歪曲[76]。

ENSimilarly, adversarial attacks pose another critical threat to MLLM agents. These attacks involve crafted inputs that exploit the model’s vulnerabilities to produce biased or undesired outputs [73]. For example, adversarial examples in multimodal settings may use subtle perturbations in images or audio to mislead the model into generating incorrect textual outputs or decision paths. Current methods to address these challenges include: • Hallucination Mitigation:Approaches such as utilizing self-feedback with visual cues to enhance model accuracy [45], employing instruction-tuning techniques to refine the model response to human instructions [50], and implementing error-correction processes that identify and rectify hallucinations within the generated text [110].

中同样，对抗性攻击对 MLLM 智能体构成了另一个严重威胁。这些攻击涉及精心设计的输入，利用模型的漏洞产生有偏见或不需要的输出[73]。例如，多模式设置中的对抗性示例可能会使用图像或音频中的微妙扰动来误导模型生成不正确的文本输出或决策路径。当前应对这些挑战的方法包括：• 幻觉缓解：利用带有视觉提示的自我反馈来提高模型准确性等方法[45]，采用指令调整技术来细化模型对人类指令的响应[50]，以及实施识别和纠正生成文本中的幻觉的纠错过程[110]。

EN• Adversarial Defense:Techniques such as adversarial training [ 115], data augmentation [112], and multimodal robustness frameworks [26] aim to improve the resilience of model against adversarial inputs. Despite these advancements, significant gaps remain, particularly in systematically evaluating the effectiveness of these mitigation strategies and understanding the trade-offs between robustness, computational efficiency, and application performance. Future research could focus on the following: • Developing unified benchmarks and evaluation metrics to assess the robustness of MLLM agents against hallucinations and adversarial attacks. • Investigating the scalability of current mitigation strategies in real-world applications, such as healthcare and autonomous systems, where multimodal inputs are critical. • Exploring privacy-preserving methods, such as differential privacy, to ensure secure handling of multimodal data.

中• 对抗性防御：对抗性训练[115]、数据增强[112]和多模态鲁棒性框架[26]等技术旨在提高模型针对对抗性输入的弹性。尽管取得了这些进步，但仍然存在重大差距，特别是在系统评估这些缓解策略的有效性以及理解鲁棒性、计算效率和应用程序性能之间的权衡方面。未来的研究可以集中在以下方面：• 制定统一的基准和评估指标，以评估MLLM 智能体针对幻觉和对抗性攻击的稳健性。• 研究当前缓解策略在现实应用中的可扩展性，例如医疗保健和自治系统，其中多模式输入至关重要。• 探索隐私保护方法，例如差分隐私，以确保多模式数据的安全处理。

ENImprovements in the safety and reliability of MLLM agents require the development of robust mechanisms to detect and mitigate vulnerabilities. These advancements will ensure that MLLM agents can function securely and effectively in diverse real-world applications, paving the way for their broader adoption and ethical deployment in AI-driven technologies.

中提高 MLLM 智能体的安全性和可靠性需要开发强大的机制来检测和缓解漏洞。这些进步将确保 MLLM 智能体能够在各种现实应用中安全有效地发挥作用，为其在人工智能驱动技术中的更广泛采用和道德部署铺平道路。

6.2.2 LLM-MA 安全与隐私 / Security and Privacy of LLM-MA System

ENAs research on LLM-MA systems increases rapidly, numerous challenges have emerged. Each agent within a multi-agent system may need to access and process sensitive data, and even execute code. Moreover, due to the intercommunication and interconnection between agents, security issues originating from a single agent can have profound and amplified effects in a multi-agent scenario. This has intensified the need for focused discussions on security and privacy issues in multi-agent environments. One of the issues is hallucination, where agents generate outputs based on incorrect or fabricated information, representing a significant challenge for both LLMs and LLM agents. This problem becomes even more complex in a multi-agent context due to the interconnected nature of these agents and their frequent communication.

中随着LLM-MA系统研究的迅速增加，出现了许多挑战。多智能体系统中的每个智能体可能需要访问和处理敏感数据，甚至执行代码。此外，由于智能体之间的相互通信和互连，源自单个智能体的安全问题可能在多智能体场景中产生深远且放大的影响。这加剧了对多智能体环境中的安全和隐私问题进行集中讨论的需要。其中一个问题是幻觉，智能体根据不正确或捏造的信息生成输出，这对LLM和LLM智能体来说都是一个重大挑战。由于这些智能体的互连性质及其频繁的通信，这个问题在多智能体环境中变得更加复杂。

ENMisinformation originating from a single agent can propagate across interconnected agents, creating a cascade of erroneous outputs throughout the system [41]. Another critical issue is the presence of malicious agents within the system. In one case, these agents may operate in a passive listening mode, where they receive information shared by other agents to perform tasks, but at the same time, they leak confidential information to attackers deliberately [9]. In another case, malicious LLM agents may engage in an active communication mode, spreading virus-infected files, phishing messages, or other malicious code, attempting to attack or disrupt other agents within the system [22]. Efforts to address these challenges have focused on two key areas:

中源自单个智能体的错误信息可以在互连的智能体之间传播，从而在整个系统中产生一系列错误输出[41]。另一个关键问题是系统中存在恶意智能体。在一种情况下，这些智能体可能以被动监听模式运行，接收其他智能体共享的信息来执行任务，但同时，它们故意向攻击者泄露机密信息[9]。在另一种情况下，恶意LLM智能体可能会采用主动通信模式，传播受病毒感染的文件、网络钓鱼消息或其他恶意代码，试图攻击或破坏系统内的其他智能体[22]。应对这些挑战的努力集中在两个关键领域：

EN• Hallucination Mitigation:It is crucial to correct errors at the individual agent level and also to manage the flow of information between agents, thereby preventing the spread of inaccurate information throughout the entire system [28]. • Malicious Agent Detection and Mitigation:Incorporating human feedback and user authorization for each step can help reduce these threats. This necessitates designing the system with robust security measures to prevent unauthorized access or misuse. An effective approach is the implementation of a stateless oracle agent, which can monitor each sensitive task and assess whether it constitutes malicious activity [77]. Despite their importance, the privacy and security challenges of LLM-MA systems remain underexplored in existing research.

中• 幻觉缓解：纠正个体智能体级别的错误以及管理智能体之间的信息流至关重要，从而防止不准确的信息在整个系统中传播[28]。• 恶意智能体检测和缓解：将人工反馈和用户授权纳入每个步骤可以帮助减少这些威胁。这就需要设计具有强大安全措施的系统，以防止未经授权的访问或误用。一种有效的方法是实现无状态预言机智能体，它可以监视每个敏感任务并评估其是否构成恶意活动[77]。尽管LLM-MA系统很重要，但其隐私和安全挑战在现有研究中仍未得到充分探讨。

ENFuture research could focus on: • Developing scalable and decentralized security solutions, such as blockchain-based communication protocols, to enhance inter-agent collaboration while minimizing systemic vulnerabilities. • Investigating privacy-preserving methodologies, including differential privacy and federated learning, to securely manage sensitive data shared among agents without compromising performance. • Creating standardized benchmarks and testing environments to systematically evaluate the robustness of LLM-MA systems against cascading misinformation and malicious activities. Currently, research on privacy and security in LLM-MA systems has not received widespread attention. However, with the rapid development of LLM-MA technology, these issues are becoming increasingly prominent. Therefore, there is an urgent need for robust security solutions to mitigate these emerging challenges.

中未来的研究可以集中在：• 开发可扩展和去中心化的安全解决方案，例如基于区块链的通信协议，以增强智能体间协作，同时最大限度地减少系统漏洞。• 研究隐私保护方法，包括差异隐私和联合学习，以安全地管理智能体之间共享的敏感数据，而不影响性能。• 创建标准化基准和测试环境，以系统地评估LLM-MA 系统针对级联错误信息和恶意活动的稳健性。目前，LLM-MA系统中的隐私和安全研究尚未受到广泛关注。然而，随着LLM-MA技术的快速发展，这些问题也日益凸显。因此，迫切需要强大的安全解决方案来缓解这些新出现的挑战。
